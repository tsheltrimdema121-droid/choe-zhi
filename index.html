What is Ikigai?

Ikigai is a Japanese concept meaning “a reason to live.” It connects four important areas of life:

  • What you love (Passion)
  • What you are good at (Skills)
  • What the world needs (Impact)
  • What you can be paid for (Income)

This project adapts Ikigai into a Bhutanese context to help students make clearer, more confident, and meaningful career decisions.
